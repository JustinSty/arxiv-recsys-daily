AGR: Agentic Generative Retrieval with Tool-Augmented Search

Ming Yang、Yaqi Yin、Zhibo Wang、Xiaoze Jiang 等 15 人

英文摘要

Large-scale search and recommendation systems derive much of their retrieval quality from rich, heterogeneous features, yet existing generative retrieval (GR) methods struggle to exploit such feature ecosystems efficiently. Static feature injection either encodes all available signals or places them according to predetermined schemes, increasing serving cost and introducing irrelevant or conflicting evidence. More fundamentally, existing methods cannot adaptively determine when a feature is needed, which feature to select, or where it should influence hierarchical Semantic ID (SID) generation. We propose Agentic Generative Retrieval (AGR), a tool-augmented paradigm that interleaves SID generation with on-demand feature acquisition. Conditioned on the request and decoded SID prefix, AGR decides whether to invoke a tool, which feature to acquire, and where its observation should influence subsequent decoding, thereby steering generation toward feature-consistent SID branches. To supervise this behavior, we construct tool-augmented trajectories based on feature applicability and information gain along the SID hierarchy, and train the model through SID pretraining followed by tool-augmented fine-tuning. Experiments on an industrial dataset and three public benchmarks, together with an online A/B test, consistently demonstrate the effectiveness of AGR.

MAPS: A Multi-Agent Personalized Script-Driven Video Advertising System Enhanced by Reinforcement Learning

Zhongyu Wang、Chen Zuo、Qingxuan Sun、Jiayang Qiao 等 7 人

英文摘要

With the rapid growth of online video consumption, video advertising has become a dominant component of digital advertising. Most personalized video advertising systems follow a retrieval-based paradigm, which matches ad content with user preferences in a feature space and recommends the most similar candidates. However, since the candidate creatives are pre-produced, this paradigm limits the granularity of personalization and leads to performance disparities across diverse user groups. To address this limitation, we propose a multi-agent personalized script-driven video advertising system (MAPS). We design four collaborative agents that form a closed-loop pipeline of user profile modeling, script generation, video synthesis, and feedback-driven iterative refinement. To train the Scripter Agent, we develop a reinforcement learning framework equipped with a multi-dimensional reward function, which steers the agent toward producing scripts that simultaneously align with user content preferences and target product information. Extensive offline experiments on representative datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves encouraging performance in generating and integrating personalized creatives. Online A/B experiments in a specific scenario in the Kuaishou advertising system show that MAPS improves CTR by 2.87%, CVR by 2.74%, and ARPU by 9.85% over the generative baseline.

Don't Rewrite the SID Map: Topology Preservation for Generative Retrieval in Dynamic E-Commerce Search

Jianbo Zhu、Xing Fang、Xiaopeng Chu、Mingmin Jin 等 8 人

英文摘要

Generative retrieval assigns each item a discrete semantic identifier (SID) and retrieves items by autoregressively generating SID sequences. In dynamic environments such as e-commerce, new items arrive continuously, requiring periodic updates to the SID-to-Item mapping and model to maintain retrieval timeliness. Each update, however, can invalidate search paths already learned by the retriever, and the quantitative relationship between mapping change and retrieval quality has not been systematically characterized. We study this problem with extensive controlled experiments over 67 days of large-scale e-commerce search logs and obtain three core findings. (1) SID semantic drift causes severe retrieval degradation that may be masked by large beam sizes: mapping updates reduce HitRate by 41.78-62.05% at beam size 1 but only 8.33-10.85% at beam size 1000, as redundant candidate generation conceals the true severity of drift. (2) The fraction of changed SIDs is strictly monotonically related to retrieval quality: as the remapped fraction increases from 0% to 72.75%, HitRate decreases monotonically, dropping up to 42.66% at beam size 1. Full remapping remains substantially worse than the preservation policy under warm-start training, indicating that short-budget continued training alone cannot repair topology disruption. (3) The SID topology-preserving principle makes even the simplest warm-start strategy highly effective: fixing old-item SIDs, inserting new-items, and warm-starting from the previous model improves old-item HitRate by 10% and new-item HitRate by 109%, validated across two non-overlapping update cycles. These findings distill into a concise deployment principle: insert new-items, freeze old-items; warm-start when possible, otherwise freeze; never change SIDs the model has already learned. A three-week online A/B test on TmallAPP shows UCTCVR +0.29% and GMV +1.16%.

Which Model for Which Role? Cost-Efficient Pareto Recommendation for LLM Agentic Workflows

Qian Xie、Wenyue Hua、Yueli He、Sripad Karne 等 10 人

英文摘要

LLM agentic workflows increasingly employ different models for specialized roles. Users may prefer different trade-offs between task quality and deployment cost, sometimes also considering latency. Finding good assignments incurs a separate search cost from evaluating multiple candidates. We introduce Cost-Coupled Gittins (CC-Gittins), a multi-objective extension of single-objective cost-aware Gittins search for Pareto recommendation. It exploits the coupling between evaluation and deployment costs: assignments that cost more to deploy also cost more to evaluate. The search alternates between seeking higher quality and lower deployment cost, reusing each evaluation to inform both searches. After each batch, CC-Gittins recommends assignments that are nondominated under uncertainty-adjusted estimates of quality and deployment cost. We evaluate CC-Gittins on eight quality--cost matrices from workflows with two to five roles and up to 1,681 candidate assignments. We also evaluate the method with latency as a third objective. Across the eight benchmarks, CC-Gittins recovers a substantial fraction of the true Pareto frontier at roughly 10% of the exhaustive-evaluation cost, and recovers most of the frontier with few false positives by 30%.

From Retrieval to Recognition:Knowledge-Enhanced Post-training of Time Series Foundation Models for Classification

Zhen Liu、Yucheng Wang、Yingpeng Du、Tianjun Wei 等 8 人

英文摘要

Time series foundation models (TSFMs) have achieved notable progress in classification tasks through large-scale pretraining. However, their post-training practice mainly focuses on implicit parameter fine-tuning, where task-relevant information is entangled with abundant irrelevant knowledge in vast parameter spaces. This may hinder TSFMs from precisely activating useful knowledge for downstream classification. To this end, we propose Retrieval-to-Recognition (R2R), a plug-and-play post-training paradigm that adapts pretrained TSFMs to time series classification. Rather than pretraining a new foundation model, R2R improves how existing TSFMs are post-trained: it introduces a decoupled query encoder to extract discriminative patterns and retrieve instance-level numerical knowledge from a constructed knowledge base. The retrieved explicit knowledge is then fused with the TSFM's implicit parametric embeddings, enabling joint knowledge transfer at both the parameter and feature levels. In this way, R2R allows TSFMs to recall task-relevant patterns learned during pretraining, improving the efficiency of knowledge utilization in downstream classification transfer. Experiments on 158 time series datasets show that R2R consistently improves the classification performance of state-of-the-art TSFMs. Ablation and knowledge-enhanced studies further validate the effectiveness of the proposed framework.

CODA: Cohort- and Drift-aware Foundation Model for Multimodal Clinical Reasoning

Changshuo Liu、Jiaqi Zhu、Wenqiao Zhang、Xiaokui Xiao 等 5 人

英文摘要

Recent advances in multimodal foundation models have demonstrated strong performance across diverse clinical tasks. However, existing approaches predominantly operate at the individual sample level and fail to model the interplay between population-level cohort patterns and patient-specific variation that underlie real-world clinical reasoning. In practice, clinicians routinely reason through patient cohorts sharing similar demographic, physiological, or pathological characteristics, while accounting for patient-specific deviations from the group prototype. Motivated by this gap, we propose CODA, a cohort- and drift-aware foundation model for multimodal clinical reasoning. CODA explicitly integrates cohort structure into the modeling pipeline through two complementary mechanisms: cohort-aware population stratification and drift-aware individual adaptation. In particular, we leverage reinforcement learning to discover clinically meaningful patient cohorts, and further model each patient's deviation from cohort prototypes as a structured drift signal encoding fine-grained individual heterogeneity. These cohort- and drift-aware representations are incorporated into the foundation model to enable reasoning that is simultaneously informed by population-level patterns and sensitive to individual-level variation. Extensive experiments on real-world electronic health record (EHR) benchmarks demonstrate that CODA achieves consistent effectiveness and strong generalizability across diverse clinical tasks, including closed-QA, open-QA, and report generation.
