LUMIR: Decoupled Multimodal Representation Learning from Heterogeneous Data for Next-POI Recommendation

Zehui Wang、Wolfram Höpken、Dietmar Jannach

英文摘要

As multimodal tourism-related information collected from heterogeneous data sources has become increasingly available, learning effective representations has attracted attention for improving recommendation performance. Existing approaches often tightly couple multimodal fusion with the architecture and optimization objective of a specific downstream model, which limits the generality and reusability of the learned representations. To address these limitations, we propose LUMIR, a decoupled multimodal representation learning framework based on contrastive learning. Multimodal information collected from heterogeneous data sources is first semantically unified into a shared textual representation space using large language models (LLMs). An interaction-aware fusion mechanism is then designed to model cross-modal interactions, while sequential signals are incorporated as a structural constraint to guide representation learning. To evaluate the learned representations, experiments are conducted on a multimodal point-of-interest (POI) dataset under the next-POI recommendation setting across multiple sequential recommendation backbones. The results show that LUMIR consistently outperforms existing multimodal fusion baselines in both accuracy-oriented and beyond-accuracy metrics, demonstrating that decoupling representation learning from downstream optimization provides an effective paradigm for learning reusable multimodal representations. The code is publicly available at https://github.com/wzehui/LUMIR.

Data Quality Challenges in Multimodal Tourism Recommender Systems

Zehui Wang、Wolfram Höpken、Dietmar Jannach

英文摘要

Modern recommender systems increasingly consider diverse data modalities to enhance model performance. However, the quality of the underlying multimodal data (e.g. data from social media platforms) has received limited attention so far. Focusing on tourism recommendation, we examine real-world challenges encountered when using datasets from Yelp and Instagram. Issues include noise from fake reviews, hallucinated content from LLMs, modality contradictions, and demographic bias. We outline future directions such as quality-aware fusion, structured and controlled LLM-based content generation to reduce hallucinations, and user group differentiation to improve the robustness and reliability of multimodal recommendation systems.

StaRSE: Compact Russian Sentence Embeddings with a Sign-Coded Static Encoder

Boris Malashenko、Ivan Jarsky、Egor Soldatov、Vladislav Luzhnov 等 9 人

英文摘要

Transformer sentence encoders provide strong retrieval quality, but their inference cost becomes a bottleneck when embeddings must be produced on CPUs or refreshed frequently. We introduce StaRSE, a compact Russian static sentence encoder that removes Transformer inference at deployment time: token directions are stored as sign bits, token norms are stored separately, and sentences are encoded by mean pooling over norm-scaled sign directions. StaRSE is initialized from ruBERT-base, fine-tuned on Russian adjacent-sentence and task-shaped pairs, and post-trained with a contrastive stage that directly optimizes the sign-projected token table. On RuMTEB, StaRSE reaches a task mean of $51.16$, $7.98$ points above the strongest included static baseline. The packaged model occupies $11.3$ MiB and has a mean CPU latency of $0.08$ ms per sentence, compared with an estimated $55.7$ MiB 16-bit parameter footprint and $1.43$ ms mean CPU latency for the closest-quality contextual encoder. Ablations examine adjacent-sentence supervision, token norms, and direct sign-projected post-training.
