KuaiRecV2: Benchmarking Large-Scale Continual Learning for Diversified and Multi-task Recommendation

Chenxu Li、Shuchang Liu、Hantao Shu、Wei Yuan 等 18 人

英文摘要

Recommender systems, which retrieve items of interest to users based on their interaction histories, have recently witnessed several key technological advancements, benefiting from the semantic representation space and model-data scaling. Nevertheless, practitioners and researchers have frequently encountered critical inconsistencies between simple offline evaluations and complex online results, and this gap still exists in the era of large models. In this work, we address several key factors that contribute to this inconsistency: 1) the \textit{large-scale continual learning challenge} that assumes a practical demand to maintain long-term model effectiveness under dynamic item pools and distributional shifts; 2) the \textit{combinatorial diversity challenge} that requires the generation strategy to solve the accuracy-diversity trade-off in both the finer token level and the holistic item level; and 3) the \textit{multi-task reward balancing challenge}, which is particularly critical for modern generative models that can accurately model the sequence distribution but are less controllable for the multi-task demands. To address these challenges and further pave the way for the development of more realistic recommendation solutions, we introduce KuaiRecV2, a deliberately constructed benchmark with industrial-level datasets from Kuaishou. Specifically, we provide a large-scale interaction dataset with million-scale multi-modal item information and long-term interaction records across 30 days, with a dynamic video candidate pool. Additionally, we provide a rigorous benchmark that evaluates retrieval accuracy, diversity, and multi-task performance in one rubric system, where an effective advancement occurs if and only if the new method improves all metrics. The code is available at https://github.com/cxli2333/KuaiRecV2

REWEAVE: Online Learning for Workload-Adaptive HNSW Indexes

Minghao Li、Nick Koudas、Raghav Mittal

英文摘要

Vector databases rely on Hierarchical Navigable Small World (HNSW) indexes for similarity search, but a graph built from data alone can be poorly matched to a skewed or changing query workload. We present REWEAVE (Reward-Guided Rewiring of Vector Graphs), an online index-maintenance method that learns edge utility from query feedback and periodically rewires the bottom layer within the original per-node degree cap. The method supports sparse exact-neighbor feedback combined with self-labels, and standard HNSW serving without learning overhead once adaptation is disabled. Under explicit assumptions we prove value-estimation and graph-stabilization guarantees. Under stationary skew, REWEAVE improves held-out recall by up to 23.3 percentage points over HNSW. In comparisons with query-aware baselines on up to 100M vectors, it achieves up to 3.3x NGFix's query throughput at recall@100 of 0.80. On Amazon-ESCI with query drift, it increases stream recall@100 from 0.745 to 0.979 with 34% fewer search distance computations than frozen HNSW. These results illustrate the potential to improve retrieval quality and reduce search effort within an existing graph-degree budget, while incurring additional work during adaptation.

ReSID: Rethinking Long-Tail Retrieval in Generative Recommendation through Item Reachability

Muyu Zou、Shunye Tang、Yi Xu、Haibo Xing 等 7 人

英文摘要

Generative recommendation (GR) represents items with discrete Semantic IDs (SIDs) and casts retrieval as sequence generation. Under skewed interaction distributions and tight serving budgets, GR struggles to retrieve long-tail items. In this work, we are the first to study long-tail reachability across the complete pipeline of SID construction, decoding, and matching. We find that under equal code lengths, parallel SID construction consistently outperforms sequential SIDs on tail items. Moreover, even when full generation fails, decoded sequences carry locally consistent information that retrieves relevant tail items via partial matching. Yet this recovery is bounded by upstream search, failing once beam search prunes all prefixes completable within a fixed mismatch tolerance. Motivated by these findings, we propose ReSID to jointly preserve and recover tail candidates. Built on long parallel SIDs, ReSID pairs popularity-debiased and user-semantic-aware beam search to keep promising paths alive with partial SID matching to recover items from surviving tokens. ReSID achieves state-of-the-art long-tail retrieval while maintaining competitive overall accuracy, with lower latency and higher throughput under narrow beams. ReSID is deployed in our production system, serving main user traffic with substantial business gains. We release our complete code and model checkpoints to support full reproducibility and real-world deployment at https://github.com/Gabriel-reina/ReSID.

TriGen: A Generative Paradigm for Trigger-Induced Recommendation

Muyu Zou、Haibo Xing、Shunye Tang、Liyan Tie 等 7 人

英文摘要

Generative recommendation typically operates under the User-Induced Recommendation (UIR) setting, predicting target items by autoregressively generating semantic IDs as a passive continuation of long-term user history. However, this paradigm overlooks Trigger-Induced Recommendation (TIR), an essential industrial scenario where a user click triggers an immediate follow-up feed. In TIR, recommendations must dynamically align with the instant trigger intent without compromising historical preference continuity. Traditional TIR methods rely on discriminative scoring and cannot guide token-level generation, while existing generative models lack mechanisms to reconcile this structural tension. To address this challenge, we propose TriGen, establishing a tailored generative paradigm for trigger-induced recommendation. TriGen introduces calibrated tail injection to re-encode local history, adaptively arbitrates between persistent tastes and immediate impulses via dynamic gating, and applies position-specific transformations to sustain level-aware trigger guidance across decoding steps. Extensive offline evaluations confirm consistent gains over competitive baselines. Furthermore, online A/B tests across six country markets demonstrate significant revenue and GMV improvements, leading to the full production deployment of TriGen.

Evaluation of Music Version Retrieval Methods for Setlist Identification in Live Concerts

Andres Ferraro、Albin Correya

英文摘要

Automatic setlist identification aims to identify the songs performed in live concert recordings by matching noisy and variable-quality audio against a database of reference tracks. This task presents challenges beyond conventional music version identification, including substantial arrangement differences, background noise, and the need to process continuous recordings efficiently at large scale. In this work, we evaluate modern music version retrieval methods for setlist identification using the ASID benchmark, comparing the established Re-MOVE system with CLEWS and NAVE, an internal neural audio version embedding model. Our results show that NAVE and CLEWS substantially outperform Re-MOVE. Notably, NAVE uses 256-dimensional embeddings, compared with 1024 dimensions for CLEWS, providing a four-fold reduction in embedding storage while achieving better retrieval performance. We further evaluate music/non-music gating and find modest improvements in detection and temporal coverage, while qualitative analysis suggests that such preprocessing can improve recognition boundaries beyond what the standard ASID metrics capture. Overall, the results demonstrate that segment-level audio embeddings provide a strong basis for scalable setlist identification and that compact representations can achieve strong accuracy under realistic live-concert conditions at much lower storage and serving cost.

When Both Sides Adapt: The Challenges of Evaluating Interactive LLM Systems

Hannah Li

英文摘要

In interactive systems where users engage with LLMs, users are adaptive and, after observing LLM responses, can change their prompting strategy, as well as the type and difficulty of subsequent tasks. Thus model responses can influence the set of prompts on which the model is subsequently evaluated. We study the implications of this user adaptivity and prompt endogeneity for the evaluation of LLMs. We define two classes of estimands for comparing models: the common-distribution effect, which compares model performance on the same distribution of prompts, and the induced-distribution effect, which compares each model on the prompt distribution generated through user interaction. We show that offline benchmark evaluation and live A/B testing target different quantities, with the standard difference-in-means estimators for the two methods converging to the common-distribution effect and the induced-distribution effect, respectively. This distinction can affect decision-making: the two comparisons can rank models differently and lead to discrepancies in deployment recommendations. We characterize when such ranking reversals occur and provide a sufficient condition under which the rankings are guaranteed to agree. This result provides a practical heuristic for assessing whether offline evaluation is sufficient to reproduce the decision suggested by a live experiment. Third, we show, in a stylized linear model of interactions, that user adaptivity can increase variance in estimation. Finally, we discuss implications of user adaptivity for the interpretation of experiments and show that adaptivity creates a common mechanism leading to common evaluation challenges, such as reward hacking and novelty effects. Together, this work highlights the necessity of measuring both model performance as well as changes in the user behavior that the model induces.
