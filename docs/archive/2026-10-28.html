PailitaoMMSearch2.0 Technical Report: Scaling Generative Retrieval to Unified End-to-End Multimodal Search

Xiaohan Ye、Zihan Gong、Jingqian Zhao、Lianyu Du 等 12 人

英文摘要

Generative retrieval models have been increasingly adopted in search and recommendation systems. However, industrial generative retrieval models are commonly specialized to a fixed online scenario: they learn from scenario-specific interactions, support a narrow set of query forms and retrieval objectives. We study how to remove these couplings and build a unified end-to-end multimodal search system for open-ended image--text queries, heterogeneous shopping intents, and online scenarios with different resource constraints. We present \textbf{PailitaoMMSearch}, which directly generates product Semantic IDs from a raw query image and free-form instruction and scales along three dimensions. \textbf{Data scaling} uses a reusable toolset that can be composed into intent- and lifecycle-specific workflows for cold-start synthesis, quality control, real-traffic refinement, and continuous data production. \textbf{Intent scaling} progressively equips one model with e-commerce product knowledge, multiple retrieval relations, and adjustable reasoning budgets through continual pre-training (CPT), mixed-budget supervised fine-tuning (SFT), and unified reinforcement learning (RL). The RL stage combines intent-balanced optimization and Sample--Beam rollouts aligned with multi-candidate serving. \textbf{Scenario scaling} exposes quality--cost operating points across model capacity and reasoning budget. The system is deployed in multimodal search applications on Taobao Pailitao. A controlled online A/B test reports statistically significant improvements, including item page views $\textbf{+17.90\%}$, transactions $\textbf{+27.04\%}$, and gross merchandise value $\textbf{+8.94\%}$.

Latent Residual Reasoning for Ranking in Generative Recommendation

Haihan Shi、Ruidong Han、Wenjie Wang、Shangyu Chen 等 11 人

英文摘要

Generative recommendation (GR) models for ranking typically adopt a fast-thinking direct prediction paradigm with strong practical efficiency. However, ranking requires fine-grained scoring across multiple candidate items, while existing GR models still mainly rely on one-shot prediction. This raises the question whether additional inference-time computation can further improve ranking quality. Recently, inspired by Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning in large language models (LLMs), some work has begun to introduce reasoning into recommendation systems. Some of these methods rely on LLM reasoning frameworks to perform explicit reasoning in textual space, but such designs are not suitable for more widely deployed non-textual recommendation models. A more relevant direction is to introduce latent reasoning into ID-based recommendation models. Although recent work has extended latent reasoning to industrial ranking, how successive reasoning steps should improve fine-grained candidate scores remains less explored. To address this problem, we propose Refiner, a latent residual reasoning framework with parameter-efficient fine-tuning for generative ranking. Our approach introduces three key components: (1) a residual reasoning mechanism that progressively refines ranking scores through cumulative additive corrections, (2) history queries that provide condensed user behavior representations to enhance candidate-specific reasoning, and (3) a parameter-efficient fine-tuning strategy that leverages LoRA modules to adapt pre-trained models for latent reasoning. Extensive experiments on two public benchmarks, an offline industrial dataset, and an online A/B test demonstrate that Refiner consistently improves over direct prediction, validating its effectiveness and practical utility.

Lossless Compression of Proximity Graph Indexes for Efficient Approximate Nearest Neighbors Search

Francesco Benocci、Leonard Dahlmann、Francesco Gargiulo、Tobias Heuer 等 8 人

英文摘要

Graph-based Approximate Nearest Neighbor (ANN) indexes, such as HNSW, are the backbone of modern vector search. However, their massive memory footprint creates a severe infrastructure bottleneck. While vector quantization is widely adopted to reduce the memory required to store data vectors, the graph's edge structure increasingly dominates total memory usage. Compressing these adjacency lists for online search presents a critical system challenge: because decompression occurs during query processing, i.e., while navigating the graph, memory savings cannot come at the expense of query latency. In this paper, we present a compression technique explicitly tailored for live HNSW routing. By applying offline graph permutations to artificially induce structural locality, we enable the use of SIMD-accelerated integer compressors, such as Stream VByte, for rapid on-the-fly decompression. Evaluated across diverse real-world workloads, we significantly outperform the current state of the art in both compression ratio and query latency. Our experimental results show that we achieve compression ratios up to 52.7% with negligible penalties in latency compared to the uncompressed baseline. Furthermore, our technique has been effectively deployed in production at eBay, achieving up to 44.3% reduction in edge storage with no degradation in query latency.

KL-MOPD: Discrepancy-Aware Multi-Objective On-Policy Distillation for Generative Recommendation

Weiqin Yang、Yandong Wang、Yuan Gao、Hao Deng 等 7 人

英文摘要

Coordinating multiple recommendation objectives (e.g., click and conversion) is critical for real-world generative recommendation (GR). However, since a positive item in GR can be retrieved only when its tokens are successively selected at every decoding step, traditional item-wise methods lack fine-grained token-wise supervision to guide intermediate steps. Motivated by its success in foundation models, we consider introducing multi-objective on-policy distillation (MOPD) into GR to distill two single-objective teachers into a student at each token. Yet, we find that in vanilla MOPD, (i) teachers exhibit unequal distillation difficulty at each token (i.e., token-level imbalance), and (ii) the harder-to-distill teacher varies across tokens (i.e., cross-token inconsistency), leaving each objective poorly learned at certain steps and hindering positive item retrieval for both objectives, leading to suboptimal performance. To address this challenge, we propose KL-MOPD (KL-Weighted Multi-Objective On-Policy Distillation), a simple yet effective distillation method that allocates token-wise teacher weights proportional to the initial student-teacher token-wise KL divergences, thereby strengthening supervision for the harder-to-distill teacher at every decoding step. Despite its simplicity, we theoretically prove that KL-MOPD strictly outperforms vanilla MOPD in the additive joint recommendation objective under mild conditions. Moreover, KL-MOPD is plug-and-play and computationally efficient, requiring only a one-time pre-training weight calibration with negligible training overhead. Extensive experiments on three public benchmarks, a billion-scale industrial dataset, and online A/B tests on our e-commerce advertising platform demonstrate that KL-MOPD significantly improves both individual and joint recommendation performance. The anonymous code is provided for peer review.

LCM-Promo: An LLM-Based Uplift Model for Promotion Allocation

Nesta Midavaine、Floris Fok、Paulo Mendes、Joao Silva 等 7 人

英文摘要

We present LCM-Promo, an LLM-based uplift model for promotion allocation. LCM-Promo is a task-specific Large Commerce Model (LCM): a finetuned LLM trained on transactional commerce data to predict user intent and promotion response. It learns monotonic promotion-response curves from production logs by interpreting single-token outputs as conversion-threshold probabilities, while conditioning on session context such as day and delivery shift. In a production A/B test on churn reactivation, LCM-Promo increased conversion by 1% and reduced promotion cost by 6% relative to the Segment-EMA production baseline.

Can Complementary Signals Bridge Similarity Islands? Manifold-Augmented Graph Embedding for Multimodal Recommendation

Zhenye Yang、Jinpeng Chen、Wenbo Fu、Jun Ma 等 9 人

英文摘要

Multimodal Recommender Systems (MMRS) leverage rich item features to mitigate data sparsity, yet similarity-driven graph construction and propagation can favor semantically similar substitutes over functionally related complements. When behavioral associations connect semantically distant items, complementary signals can remain geometrically isolated, yielding fragmented "Similarity Islands" and semantic drift when standard propagation is applied. To overcome these limitations, we propose MAGE (Manifold-Augmented Graph Embedding), a framework for personalized multimodal ranking that decouples complementarity modeling into offline geometric warping and online topological propagation. The offline phase enhances item representations by extracting complement-aware residual directions via a Soft Conceptor mechanism and injecting them under a local gap-preserving constraint, incorporating co-purchase evidence while controlling the magnitude of geometric perturbation. Synergistically, the online phase employs an asymmetric dual-stream GNN that treats visual features as stable anchors and augmented textual features as flexible probes, fused through a self-calibrated cross-modal mechanism to reconcile shared semantics and modality-specific residual cues. Extensive experiments on four Amazon datasets show that MAGE yields consistent improvements over strong multimodal recommendation baselines. Together with the supporting analyses in the appendix, these results suggest that geometry-aware augmentation can alleviate the semantic mismatch that arises in complementary recommendation settings. Our code is available at https://github.com/Alex-bupt/mage.

RPSD: Retrieval-Privileged On-Policy Self-Distillation with Optimal Transport

Dongyue Li、Zebang Cheng、Zhongyi Deng、Hongboxu 等 6 人

英文摘要

Sequential recommendation predicts a user's next interaction from historical behavior. Recent LLM-based methods recast this task as generative recommendation and increasingly adopt reinforcement learning such as GRPO for post-training. However, these methods suffer from two limitations, namely credit-blind optimization, where a sequence-level reward is shared across all generated tokens, and collaborative-blind supervision, where each training signal is derived from a single user and ignores behavioral patterns shared across similar users. To address both issues, we propose Retrieval-Privileged on-policy Self-Distillation (RPSD). For credit-blind optimization, we introduce on-policy self-distillation (OPSD), in which a teacher with privileged knowledge re-evaluates the student's on-policy response, and the token-level confidence difference between the student and teacher reshapes the shared sequence-level advantage into token-specific credit. However, the privileged knowledge in vanilla OPSD is still derived from a single user, leaving collaborative-blind supervision unresolved. We therefore ground the teacher in collaborative behavioral evidence from similar users. Specifically, we use temporally aware optimal transport (OT) to compare interaction trajectories across users by jointly considering item similarity and temporal structure, and further apply diversity-aware selection to retain complementary trajectories as privileged evidence. In this way, on-policy self-distillation provides fine-grained token-level credit, while OT enriches the teacher with structured cross-user evidence. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first work to bring on-policy self-distillation to sequential recommendation and to use cross-user behavior as its privileged signal. Extensive experiments across multiple real-world datasets demonstrate that RPSD achieves state-of-the-art performance.

Rethinking Positional Encoding and Interaction Granularity for CTR Prediction

Xinxun Zhang、Yuxiang Wang、Xiaokai Zhou、Jiayue Li 等 11 人

英文摘要

Click-through rate (CTR) prediction relies on historical behaviors and contextual features to estimate clicks on a target item. Existing Transformer-based CTR models often adopt index-based or temporal positional encodings and token-level causal interaction. However, we observe that most existing positional encodings do not outperform NoPE, while relaxing causal constraints within target-relative temporal groups also yields gains. These observations motivate a fundamental question: what positional encoding and interaction granularity does CTR prediction actually require? To address this question, we propose \textbf{TRIP}, which combines target-aware rank--time positional encoding with temporal-group interaction modeling. Specifically, Factorized Rank Encoding represents each behavior's ordinal position within the historical sequence through a low-rank factorization, while Target-Relative Time Encoding characterizes its temporal distance to the target on a coarse-to-fine scale. This joint design grounds each behavior's positional representation in its sequence context and temporal relation to the current target. Furthermore, Temporal Group-Causal Interaction enriches intra-group representations through bidirectional interaction and masked reconstruction while preserving inter-group causal order. Finally, behavior representations are condensed into compact group summaries for efficient group-level modeling in subsequent layers. Experiments on four public benchmarks show that TRIP improves AUC by up to 4.45\% relative to the strongest baseline.

Algorithmic Monoculture in Machine Learning-based Site-Specific Fertilizer Recommendation

Abdelghani Belgaid

英文摘要

Machine learning recommendations are often evaluated one model at a time, despite deployment across interconnected decision makers. For continuous NPK fertilizer prescriptions, correlated errors can aggregate across farms and generate systemic costs. A model of shared and farm-specific recommendation errors links expected social loss to model adoption shares; the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index characterizes concentration effects when common model errors are independent and predictive risks are equal. A covariance-generalized formulation captures the dependence between model families. A two-model threshold characterizes when privately optimal monoculture is socially dominated, and a convex portfolio rule limits average private predictive regret. Nine model families across five Moroccan cereal systems provide a benchmark-based calibration. Under illustrative parameters and a 10% regret cap, accuracy-constrained diversification lowers modeled social loss in all five systems by 10.89-41.53% (mean 19.41%). Uniform allocation reduces modeled loss in this setting, but breaches the regret cap in every crop. Correlation sensitivity shows that highly dependent model errors attenuate the benefits of diversification. These counterfactual outcomes are not estimates of agronomic or monetary welfare; the framework identifies adoption architecture and error dependence as ecosystem-level evaluation variables.

HorizON: A Multi-Horizon Deep Operator Network for Property Prediction of Organic Battery Stacks

Subhash V. S. Ganti、Lukas Woelfel、Arla Diengdoh、Tzvetan Mihaylov 等 7 人

英文摘要

Transition-metal-based batteries rely on critical elements such as lithium, cobalt, and nickel. Their extraction creates environmental and supply-chain challenges. Organic electrode materials (OEMs) reduce this reliance and provide broad molecular tunability. However, OEM-based batteries still show limited long-term cycling stability. Their design also requires the joint optimization of coupled metrics such as voltage, specific capacity, capacity retention, and energy density. These metrics depend on the full battery stack and on the interactions among its components rather than on the active material alone. In our previous work, we developed a data-fusion multi-task framework for average discharge voltage and first-cycle specific capacity. Both targets are single-valued. Here, we extend that framework to multi-horizon prediction by predicting a property at every cycle of a discharge trajectory rather than at a single cycle. Our multi-fidelity and Multi-horizon Deep Operator Network learns DFT redox potentials and cycle-dependent specific-capacity trajectories within one model. We train it on complete battery-stack representations and usage conditions, so it learns battery-stack interactions rather than cathode-only effects. This framework supports long-term property prediction, inverse design, and process optimization for organic battery systems.

主题：multi-task、one model
